About {{ app_name }}

One authoritative system for the complete lifecycle of Bank Guarantees.

{{ app_name }} manages the complete lifecycle of Bank Guarantees (BGs) for our multi-business-unit organization - replacing a fragmented stack of spreadsheets, email approval threads and manual SAP cross-referencing with one authoritative platform.

Every approver sees an already-assembled picture of the facts before making a decision. AI accelerates the work but never silently finalizes anything of legal or financial consequence, and every deadline is tracked by policy timers rather than human memory.

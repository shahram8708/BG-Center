Data protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy describes how {{ app_name }} collects, uses and protects information. This is an internal enterprise platform; access is restricted to authorised employees.

What we collect

  • Account information: your work email, full name, roles, business unit, and sign-in activity.
  • Bank Guarantee data: documents and metadata you upload or create for legitimate business purposes.
  • Usage logs: audit events recording who performed which action, and when, to support governance and security.

How we use it

  • To operate the platform and manage the Bank Guarantee lifecycle.
  • To enforce role-based access and delegation-of-authority controls.
  • To provide AI-assisted analysis on documents you upload.
  • To send you legitimate notifications about the guarantees you work on.

Retention

Records are retained for as long as they are needed for the legitimate business purposes described above and to satisfy legal, regulatory and audit requirements. Access to sensitive data is limited to personnel with a legitimate need.

Security

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, hashed credentials, role-based access control, and a complete audit trail of privileged actions. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Your responsibilities

You must keep your credentials confidential, only upload documents for legitimate business purposes, and not attempt to access information outside your authorised role.
